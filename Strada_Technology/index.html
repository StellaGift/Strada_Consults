STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

A quick guide to our technology services and how we work together.

What services does Strada Technology provide?

We provide custom software development, VAPT, cybersecurity training, cloud security and DevSecOps, identity and access management, security monitoring and incident response, AI solutions, business process automation, secure payment systems, and AI-powered website and CMS solutions.

Who do you work with?

We work with organisations looking to build digital products, improve existing systems, or strengthen security. We first discuss your needs and confirm whether our services and experience fit the work.

How does a project get started?

We begin with a conversation about your goals, users, current setup, and constraints. If there is a good fit, we define the scope, expected deliverables, timeline, and next steps in writing.

Can you build software around our existing processes?

Yes. We learn how the work is done today, identify the needs the software should address, and plan a solution around your users, workflows, and systems.

What is included in a VAPT engagement?

After written authorisation and scope agreement, we assess the agreed systems using controlled testing, document findings, and provide practical remediation guidance. Testing stays within the approved scope.

Can you help secure our cloud environment and development process?

We can review cloud configuration and development workflows, identify security gaps, and help plan improvements. The work is scoped to your environment, priorities, and operating requirements.

Do you support identity and access management?

Yes. We help organisations plan access controls so people can reach the systems they need, with permissions aligned to their roles and responsibilities.

What kinds of AI solutions do you build?

Depending on the use case, we can develop chatbots, integrate AI into websites and workflows, or engineer custom AI features. We discuss data, security, oversight, and limitations as part of scoping.

Can you create a secure website or content management system?

We design and build websites and CMS solutions around your content, users, and business needs, with appropriate security considerations and optional AI integrations.

How do you keep us informed during the work?

We agree on communication and review points when the project is scoped, share progress against milestones, and raise decisions or risks that need your attention.
