Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how Strada Consults and Services Limited (“Strada”, “we”, “us” or “our”), including its technology brand Strada Technology & Digital Solutions Limited, handles personal information when you visit this website or contact our team. It is intended to describe the site as it currently operates.

1. Information we handle

  • Messages you send us: if you email us, we receive the address and any name, contact details, project information or other personal information you include. This website does not currently have an online enquiry form.
  • Technical request information: when a page is requested, the browser and hosting infrastructure may process information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, requested page, browser or device details, and request time.
  • Theme preference: if you change the site’s dark or light mode, the preference is saved in your browser’s local storage so it can be remembered. It is not sent to us by the site.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information by email unless it is necessary and we have explained how it will be handled.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, discuss and provide requested services, maintain and protect the website, address technical issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. Where required, we rely on an appropriate lawful basis under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, such as taking steps at your request before entering a contract, performing a contract, meeting a legal obligation, or pursuing a legitimate interest that does not override your rights.

3. Cookies and external services

This site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. Its theme preference is stored in browser local storage as described above. The site loads the Montserrat font from Google Fonts. When your browser requests that font, Google may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details and handle it under Google’s Privacy Policy.

If you follow a link to another website or open an email application, that service operates under its own privacy terms. We do not control its processing.

4. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. We may make relevant information available to service providers that help us host, secure, maintain or communicate through the site, and where disclosure is required by law or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Those providers may process information in Nigeria or another country. Where applicable, we take the steps required by law for such processing and transfers.

5. Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to your enquiry, managing a business relationship, meeting legal requirements and resolving disputes. The period may depend on the nature of the information and our obligations. We apply appropriate safeguards, but no website, email system or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and its conditions, you may ask us to confirm whether we process your personal information, access it, correct it, erase it, restrict or object to certain processing, or provide it in a portable format where that right applies. If processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw it. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To make a request, email stradaconsults@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. We may ask for information to verify your identity and understand your request. You can find information about the Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.

7. Services provided to clients

This policy covers information handled through this website and related enquiries. If we process personal information for a client as part of a project, the relevant service agreement and any applicable data-processing terms describe that work.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to stradaconsults@gmail.com.
