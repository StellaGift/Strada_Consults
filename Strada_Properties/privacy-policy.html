Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Strada Properties Limited collects, uses, protects, and shares information when you visit this website, make an inquiry, request a listing review, upload property media, or communicate with our team.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number, and message content.
  • Property inquiry details, listing information, location preferences, budgets, and transaction interests.
  • Uploaded property media, documents, photographs, videos, and supporting files you choose to send.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and usage patterns.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, assess property requests, arrange follow-up, verify listing materials, improve our website, communicate service updates, and support real estate transactions or advisory work requested by you.

Legal Basis and Data Protection

Where applicable, we process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and related data protection principles. We aim to collect only information that is relevant to the service request and to handle it with reasonable care.

Sharing and Service Providers

We may share limited information with trusted service providers that support website forms, file uploads, communication, inspection, documentation, or transaction follow-up. We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, lawful request, or to protect legitimate rights and safety.

Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for inquiry handling, service delivery, record keeping, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and business administration.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you provided, subject to lawful retention needs. You may also ask us to stop non-essential communication.
