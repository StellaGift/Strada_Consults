Clear Answers Before You Move

Real estate decisions deserve straight answers. These FAQs focus on the property questions clients ask us most often about buying, selling, valuation, management, development, and diaspora investment.

How do I buy or sell a property with Strada Properties Limited?

Our team guides you through every step, from property search or listing, negotiation, verification, and closing, so the transaction stays clear, documented, and secure.

How is property valuation determined?

We assess market trends, location, property condition, title strength, development potential, and comparable sales to provide reliable valuation guidance.

Do you manage rental properties?

Yes. We support property management needs such as tenant sourcing, lease administration, maintenance coordination, and owner reporting.

Can you assist with property development projects?

Absolutely. We can support planning, design coordination, construction oversight, site updates, and strategic market analysis from concept to delivery.

Can you manage properties for diasporans?

Definitely. We help clients abroad acquire, develop, and manage properties with structured updates, virtual tours, digital documentation, and clear accountability.

Do you offer investment advice for real estate?

Yes. Our consultancy helps investors identify opportunities, assess risk, compare locations, and build strategies aimed at long-term value and practical returns.

What should I know before buying off-plan or newly built property?

Review the developer's track record, title documentation, building approvals, delivery timeline, payment milestones, and defect liability terms before committing funds.

How do you help clients avoid fraudulent property transactions?

We prioritize due diligence: ownership checks, document review, physical inspection, seller verification, and clear transaction records before commitments are made.

Can I inspect a property virtually before traveling?

Yes. Where available, we provide clear photos, video clips, virtual walkthroughs, location details, and follow-up notes so remote clients can review options with confidence.

How long does a purchase or sale usually take?

Timelines vary by property type, document readiness, due diligence, negotiation, and payment structure. We keep clients informed at each stage so delays are visible early.
