About Strada Properties Limited

  1. Home

Built for Property Decisions That Need Trust

Strada Properties Limited helps clients move from interest to ownership with verified guidance, disciplined execution, and clear communication at every stage.

Strada Properties Limited is the real estate arm of Strada Consults and Services Limited, created to help families, investors, property owners, and Nigerians in the diaspora buy, sell, manage, and develop property with confidence.

Our work covers real estate brokerage, acquisition support, seller representation, valuation and survey coordination, documentation review, property management, and construction supervision. Every service is shaped around one promise: protect the client's interest before, during, and after the transaction.

For clients outside Nigeria, we provide a structured path to ownership and development through property search, inspection feedback, documentation guidance, transaction follow-up, site monitoring, and clear communication. The goal is simple: reduce uncertainty, improve accountability, and make each decision easier to trust.

Real estate is more than a listing or plot number. It is a financial decision, a family milestone, and often a long-term investment. We combine market insight, property verification, negotiation support, and practical supervision into one coordinated client experience.

Our Vision

A trusted property path for clients at home and abroad.

To become a leading property partner for secure acquisition, transparent development, and long-term real estate value across Nigeria.

Our Mission

Make property ownership clearer, safer, and better managed.

We guide every client with verified opportunities, practical advisory, documentation discipline, responsive communication, and accountable on-ground execution.

Our Values

Integrity, clarity, diligence, and measurable client value.

We value honest guidance, careful verification, consistent updates, strong documentation, and decisions that protect the client's interest beyond the closing date.
